Your clubs, your alerts

Choose up to three clubs and follow up to two matches at a time. Then decide what is worth a buzz: goals, cards, substitutions, kick-off, full time. Each one is a switch.

You can search every club in Europe's top leagues by country, or by name.

The table, as it stands

Premier League, La Liga, Bundesliga, Ligue 1 and the Champions League. The top four are marked, and your own clubs are lit up so you find them fast.

A whole season, one tap away

Open any club to see every result and every fixture still to come, month by month. Tap a match for the timeline, the line-ups and the stats.

What it is, and what it isn't

Near-live, not instant
Scores refresh every few minutes while matches are on. It is built to keep you informed, not to beat the broadcast.
Five competitions
Premier League, La Liga, Bundesliga, Ligue 1 and the Champions League.
Yours to delete
One tap in the app removes your account, your clubs and your devices.
